QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Does the Skin Finder diagnose me?

No. It only provides general product guidance based on your answers.

Can I use it for vitiligo?

Yes. The site routes vitiligo enquiries to Herbal Force instead of claiming that a product cures or treats it.

How does EFT work?

The site creates an order reference and displays the business's bank details. The customer transfers the amount and Herbal Force verifies the payment before fulfilling the order.

Can I pay by card?

V11 uses EFT by default so there is no payment-gateway transaction fee. A card gateway can be added later.
